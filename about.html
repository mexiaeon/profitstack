About AgencyStack Guide

Clearer software decisions for modern businesses.

Independent software research · Editorial policy

AgencyStack Guide is an independent publication creating practical guides to business software for small agencies, freelancers, and web designers. We help readers understand product categories, compare workflows, and identify the questions worth asking before paying for a subscription.

AgencyStack Guide is the agency-software publication that grew out of the original ProfitStack project. We are keeping the existing site structure and useful page URLs so earlier research remains available while our coverage becomes more specialized.

What we cover

Our focus is customer relationship management, client onboarding, marketing automation, email marketing, and website tools for small agencies, freelancers, and web designers.

How our pages are prepared

Initial AgencyStack Guide pages are research-based overviews prepared from publicly available product and program information. They are not represented as hands-on product tests. We do not publish invented star ratings, user testimonials, performance benchmarks, or guaranteed savings. Where feature availability or price can change, readers should confirm details on the provider's official website.

Editorial independence

Some outbound links may become affiliate links. Affiliate compensation may support the publication, but it does not make a product suitable for every reader. We aim to explain relevant limitations and alternatives, not just benefits.

Corrections and questions

For corrections, product updates, or editorial questions, use the contact page. We will add a working contact email before public launch.
