GetResponse vs. HubSpot: which type of platform do you need?

Comparison guide · Choose based on the job you need done

These products overlap in parts of the marketing journey, but they are not identical choices. GetResponse is commonly approached as an email-marketing and automation platform. HubSpot is a broader customer platform built around CRM data and connected sales, marketing, and service tools.

At a glance

ConsiderationGetResponseHubSpot
Core starting pointEmail campaigns and marketing automationCRM and connected customer workflows
Potential fitNewsletter, subscriber, and campaign-focused workTeams coordinating leads, sales, and customer activity
Key buying checkContact counts, send limits, automation tiersHub selection, seats, contacts, onboarding, feature tiers
Complexity to assessCampaign and automation needsHow much of the wider platform your team will use

Choose GetResponse if…

Your immediate priority is building and communicating with an email audience, and the plan includes the automation and list-management features you need.

Consider HubSpot if…

You need a shared customer database and expect marketing, sales, or service workflows to work from the same CRM foundation.

Before you decide

  • List the three workflows you must support today.
  • Estimate contacts, users, and monthly activity for the next year.
  • Check total cost at your expected usage—not just the entry price.
  • Test importing, integrations, reporting, and cancellation requirements.
