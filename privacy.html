Privacy policy

AgencyStack Guide currently does not operate user accounts, accept payments, or collect newsletter subscriptions. The directory search and software finder operate in your browser and do not send your answers to us.

External websites

When you follow a link to a software provider or another third-party website, that site may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms. Review those policies before sharing personal information.

Future changes

Optional analytics: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is loaded only after you select “Accept analytics” in the consent banner. If you accept, Google may use cookies or similar technologies to collect page views, browser and device details, approximate location, and interactions with external provider links. If you reject analytics, the GA4 script is not loaded. Your choice is saved in this browser using local storage. You can change your choice at any time using “Analytics settings” at the bottom of the site. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.
