GetResponse: email marketing and automation overview

Category: Email marketing · Research-based overview

GetResponse is a marketing platform built around email campaigns and automation, with additional tools that may include landing pages, forms, and conversion-focused features. It is worth evaluating when your main goal is to build an audience and communicate with subscribers.

Primary useEmail campaigns
Key checkContact and send limits
Before payingMap your automation needs

A look inside GetResponse

Who may find it useful?

  • Small businesses building an email list.
  • Creators or publishers sending regular newsletters.
  • Teams that want automated follow-ups based on subscriber actions.

What to compare before paying

  • How the plan counts contacts and email sends.
  • Which automation workflows are included at each tier.
  • Landing page, form, and integration limits.
  • Whether billing changes as your subscriber list grows.
  • Cancellation, refund, and support terms.

Questions to ask

Can you import your current contacts? Are the integrations you need included? Can you test the workflow on a free or trial plan before committing?
