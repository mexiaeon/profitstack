GetResponse: email marketing and automation overview

Category: Email marketing · Research-based overview

GetResponse is a marketing platform built around email campaigns and automation, with additional tools that may include landing pages, forms, and conversion-focused features. It is worth evaluating when your main goal is to build an audience and communicate with subscribers.

Primary useEmail campaigns
Key checkContact and send limits
Before payingMap your automation needs

A look inside GetResponse

Who may find it useful?

  • Small businesses building an email list.
  • Creators or publishers sending regular newsletters.
  • Teams that want automated follow-ups based on subscriber actions.

What to compare before paying

  • How the plan counts contacts and email sends.
  • Which automation workflows are included at each tier.
  • Landing page, form, and integration limits.
  • Whether billing changes as your subscriber list grows.
  • Cancellation, refund, and support terms.

Questions to ask

Can you import your current contacts? Are the integrations you need included? Can you test the workflow on a free or trial plan before committing?

Advantages and trade-offs

Potential advantages

  • Combines email campaigns, signup forms, and landing pages
  • Automation options can support welcome sequences and follow-ups
  • Higher tiers add broader marketing and creator tools

Things to watch

  • Plan features and automation allowances differ by tier
  • Pricing depends on list size and billing period
  • Advanced ecommerce, webinar, and creator features may require higher plans

Who should consider an alternative?

If you mainly need a lightweight newsletter tool for a small list, compare MailerLite and Brevo before paying for a broader platform. If your main requirement is a shared sales database and pipeline management, compare CRM-focused options such as HubSpot.

Pricing and plan considerations

GetResponse currently lists Starter, Marketer, Creator, and Enterprise plans. The feature set expands by tier, and the quoted price depends on billing period and contact-list size. Starter includes one custom automation workflow; higher plans add broader automation, ecommerce, webinar, website, and creator capabilities. Check the live price for your list size before subscribing.

Frequently asked questions

Is GetResponse only for email newsletters?

No. Its current plans extend into automation, landing pages, ecommerce-related workflows, webinars, and creator tools. Which features you can use depends on your plan.

What should I check before choosing a plan?

Confirm your contact count, required automation workflows, sending needs, team seats, integrations, and billing period. Check the exact plan limits rather than relying on a general feature list.

Is GetResponse a good fit for a sales CRM?

It can support marketing workflows, but if your central need is managing a sales pipeline and shared customer records, compare it with a CRM-first platform such as HubSpot.
