HubSpot: CRM and customer platform overview

Category: CRM, sales, and marketing · Research-based overview

HubSpot offers a connected customer platform built around CRM data, with products and features for marketing, sales, service, content, and operations. It may suit businesses that want customer information and team workflows organized in one ecosystem.

Primary useCRM and customer workflows
Key checkSeats and feature tiers
Before payingList your team's core processes

Who may find it useful?

  • Small teams that need a shared view of leads and customers.
  • Businesses coordinating marketing and sales activity.
  • Growing organizations that expect to add processes and users over time.

What to compare before paying

  • Which Hub or product edition contains the features you need.
  • Seat, contact, automation, and usage limits.
  • Onboarding fees or required commitments.
  • Whether your team will actually use the broader platform.
  • Data migration and integration requirements.

Questions to ask

Can you start with the CRM features you need today? What happens when you add users or exceed plan limits? Which tools are bundled and which require separate subscriptions?
